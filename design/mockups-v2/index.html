FAQ

Questions funders ask.

Anything else? A coordinator can walk you through the sandbox demo.

Does the AI decide who gets paid?

No. The agent drafts. A deterministic policy engine decides whether each draft is allowed, and a coordinator has to approve the run before PayPal sends anything.

How does a family receive the money?

PayPal Payouts sends it to the family’s PayPal email. They get a private link by SMS that shows the amount and status, and how to move it to GCash or a bank.

Can I see where my contribution went?

Yes. The public page shows your pool: what you put in, which storms and towns it paid for, and what’s left. Each payout is split pro-rata across the pools that cover the family.

What stops a family being paid twice?

A family paid in the last 7 days can’t be drafted again under any storm name, and each PayPal batch ID comes from the run ID, so a retried approval can never pay twice.

Is this real money?

Not in the demo. It runs on the PayPal sandbox with simulated bulletins for a fictional Typhoon Dalisay.
